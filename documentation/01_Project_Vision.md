 Smart School Management System

## Project Overview

The Smart School Management System is a web-based application that helps schools manage their daily operations digitally.

It provides separate dashboards for Admin, Teachers, and Students.

The system manages students, teachers, attendance, exams, results, fees, homework, notices, and reports from one platform.

The main goal is to reduce paperwork, save time, improve accuracy, and make school management easier.

Future versions will include AI features, QR attendance, online fee payment, and advanced analytics.

---

## Project Objectives

- Digitalize school management
- Reduce paperwork
- Manage student records
- Manage teacher records
- Record attendance
- Manage exams and results
- Manage fee collection
- Share notices
- Generate reports
- Build a scalable system for future enhancements
